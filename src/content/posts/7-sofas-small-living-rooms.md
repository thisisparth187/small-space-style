## Measure first — 30 seconds

- **Wall:** measure it, subtract 36 in for the walkway and a side table.
- **Door:** measure your narrowest doorway — the sofa's diagonal depth has to clear it, not its packaged width.
- **Coffee table:** leave 18 in between it and the sofa.

## Pick in 10 seconds

Buy the **72-inch apartment sofa** unless you have a specific reason not to. Room under 11 ft wide? Go **68-inch loveseat** or **66-inch armless**. Kids or pets? Go **74-inch washable**. Guests actually sleep over? Go **70-inch sleeper**.

## Before you order

- Check the **overall width in inches** on the retailer listing — not a review, not memory.
- Check **seat depth (20–22 in)** and **seat height (17–19 in)**.
- Check the **return policy** — who pays return shipping on a 90-pound sofa?
